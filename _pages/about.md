Tenured Professor for Human-Centered AI and Clinical Decision Support Systems (since 09/26) at the Faculty of Computer Science at [Heilbronn University of Applied Sciences](https://www.hs-heilbronn.de/de/stefan.ehrlich). Previously research group leader (04/23-08/26) at [SETLabs Research GmbH](https://www.setlabs.de/) in Munich and postdoctoral research fellow and visiting scientist (11/21-10/25) at [Harvard Medical School](https://simonyanlab.meei.harvard.edu/) in Boston. PhD (Dr.-Ing.) in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the [Technical University of Munich](https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/ics/home/) in 2020 on neuroadaptive human-robot interaction.

My research interests lie at the intersection of human-centered artificial intelligence, medical AI, robotics, and neurotechnologies. I am equally dedicated to teaching and mentoring, with a strong focus on connecting fundamental concepts with real-world applications.


### Research Interests
* Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence
* Medical AI and Clinical Decision Support
* Human-Centered Robotics
* Multimodal and Neuro-Symbolic AI
* Virtualization, Simulation, and Digital Twins
