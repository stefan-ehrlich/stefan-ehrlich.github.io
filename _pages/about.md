I am Professor for Human-Centered AI and Clinical Decision Support Systems (since 09/26) at the Faculty of Computer Science at [Heilbronn University of Applied Sciences](https://www.hs-heilbronn.de/de/stefan.ehrlich). My research interests lie at the intersection of human-centered artificial intelligence, robotics, and neurotechnologies. I design trustworthy intelligent systems that effectively support human decision-making in healthcare and other high-stakes domains. 

I am equally dedicated to teaching and mentoring, with a strong focus on connecting fundamental concepts with real-world applications and enabling students to critically engage with the opportunities, risks, and societal implications of AI and related technologies.

Previously I was a research group leader (04/23-08/26) at [SETLabs Research GmbH](https://www.setlabs.de/) in Munich focusing on applied research into AI for healthcare and human-centered robotics for clinical workflow automation. I was also a Postdoctoral Research Fellow and Visiting Scientist (11/21-10/25) at [Harvard Medical School](https://simonyanlab.meei.harvard.edu/) in Boston, where I developed a brain-computer interface system for treatment of neurological voice disorders. 

I completed my PhD (Dr.-Ing.) and in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the [Technical University of Munich](https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/ics/home/) in 2020 on neuroadaptive human-robot interaction.

I am driven by deep curiosity and a desire to create meaningful innovation. I always appreciate connecting with researchers and tech-enthusiasts who are working along similar lines. If any of this resonates with you, I am happy to [connect!](mailto:stefan.kh.ehrlich@gmail.com)

### Interests
* Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence
* Medical AI and Clinical Decision Support
* Robotics and Autonomous Systems
* Multimodal and Neuro-Symbolic AI
* Virtualization, Simulation, and Digital Twins
